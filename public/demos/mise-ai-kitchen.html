Four things people ask before they point a camera at their fridge

01 Does the photo leave my phone?

No. Recognition runs on the device and the picture is discarded the moment the ingredient list comes back — what syncs is the list of names, not the image. You can turn even that off and keep everything local, which costs you the shared household plan and nothing else.

02 What happens when it gets an ingredient wrong?

It shows you how sure it is and asks about anything under 80%. Correct it once and it remembers the shape of your kitchen — a fortnight in, most people's scans come back clean.

03 Do I have to plan a whole week?

No. Plenty of people only ever use the scan and the question box, which is the whole free tier working as intended. The week plan exists for households that would rather decide once.

04 Is any of this real?

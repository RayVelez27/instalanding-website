Six things this rail can be

Same markup, same keyframe. Only the custom properties and a modifier class change.

The one line that matters: the keyframe ends at translateX(calc(-100% - var(--gap))), not -100%. The group is 100% wide, but the flex gap adds space between the two copies — miss it and the strip jumps one gap every cycle, which reads as a dropped frame and gets blamed on the browser.

Opposed bands

Axis

Vertical rail

Flip the flex direction, swap the keyframe to translateY, give the rail a height. Nothing else moves.

Ticker

Status line

Railed band, small caps, a long duration. Slow is the difference between a ticker and a distraction.

Same trick, other axis

Stand it on its end

A vertical rail is the horizontal one with flex-direction: column, a height, and translateY in the keyframe. The mask moves to the top and bottom edges.

Give a vertical rail an explicit height. Without one the group has nothing to be 100% of, the animation travels zero pixels, and it fails silently.

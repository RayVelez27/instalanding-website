One road. Every corgi dances.

Corgi Road is a walk, not a race. There is no combat, no timer and no map, only a road running west through blossom, rice fields and level crossings, and the corgis dancing along it.

In-engine

The long road west

Twelve kilometres of hills, blossom tunnels and level crossings, built as one continuous walk. No loading screens, no waypoints, only the road.

In-engine

Dancing is the only verb

Every corgi on the road dances, yours included. Walk to dance faster, stand still to dance slower. That is the whole moveset.

In-engine

Something very large

At the western end stands a cherry tree older than the road, eighty metres of blossom on one trunk. Nobody knows who planted it. You will want to get closer.

Questions people ask.

Is there any combat?

No. The corgi does not fight. It dances. Other corgis dance back.

What is at the end of the road?

A cherry tree eighty metres tall, older than the road and always in bloom. What happens when you reach it is the one thing we will not put in a trailer.

Can I pet the other dogs?

Every single one. Walk up to a dancer and hold the button; they dance with you for a while, then go back to their spot on the road.

How long is it?

The road takes about two hours at a walk. Most people take longer, because the petals are hidden and the corgis are distracting.

Is the demo on this page the real game?

It is a small slice: one loop of road, a few hundred trees and twenty-four petals. The full game is one continuous road with no loop.

The network

Distance is the part of latency you cannot optimise away.

You can quantise the model, batch the requests and rewrite the kernel, and the packet still has to cross an ocean. Proxima moves the model instead.

Two hundred places

One deploy lands everywhere at once. Requests resolve to the nearest healthy box without you writing a routing table or maintaining one.

Weights stay warm

A region that has served your model keeps it resident. Adding the two hundred and fifteenth costs a cold start once, not on every call.

Fails to the next ring

A region going dark is a routing event, not an outage. The request lands one ring further out and the caller sees a slower answer, not an error.

Push the artifact

One build, whatever you already produce. Proxima does not need your training code, your dataset or your framework of the month.

Pick a footprint

One region, a continent, or everywhere. It is a number in a config, and changing it is a deploy rather than a migration.

Point your clients at one host

The same hostname everywhere. Anycast puts the call on the nearest edge and the answer comes back from there.
